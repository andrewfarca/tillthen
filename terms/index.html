1. Community Rules

Don't post, send, or do any of the following anywhere in Till Then — in a memory, a caption, a chat, a name, a capsule name, or a profile:

  • Anything sexual involving anyone under 18, ever — including "jokes", drawings, or AI-generated images. We remove it, preserve it as the law requires, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and ban the account.
  • Intimate images of anyone without their consent — including real or AI-made nude or sexual images, and threats to share them ("sextortion").
  • Nudity or sexual content of any kind. Till Then is used by teenagers.
  • Bullying, harassment, humiliation, or threats — including ganging up on someone in a group capsule or chat.
  • Hate or slurs targeting people for their race, ethnicity, religion, national origin, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or serious illness.
  • Violence, or content that promotes, glorifies or encourages self-harm, suicide or eating disorders.
  • Selling or promoting drugs, weapons, or anything illegal; scams, spam or fake giveaways.
  • Pretending to be someone else — including Till Then or its staff — or using a name, username or profile meant to mislead.
  • Filming or photographing people where they'd expect privacy (bathrooms, changing rooms) or people who clearly didn't agree to be recorded.
  • Sharing someone's private information (address, phone number, passwords) without their permission.
  • Breaking the app: hacking, scraping, automating accounts, getting around a block or a ban, or getting around the time lock.

Names have extra rules, because people who aren't your friends can see them: usernames and display names can't include swear words, slurs, sexual terms, links, emails, @handles, phone numbers, fake check marks, or anything that looks like Till Then. Usernames can be changed once every 30 days, and an old username is held for a while so nobody can grab it to pass as you.

We check text automatically for slurs and sexual terms and won't post a message, caption or name that contains them. Ordinary swearing between friends in a chat is fine; it isn't in a name.

2. Reporting, and how we act

  • In the app: tap ⋯ on a memory, press and hold a chat message, or open someone's profile, and choose Report. You can also Block anyone: they can't find you, add you, message you or invite you. Reports are anonymous — the person reported is never told who reported them.
  • We review every report within 24 hours, and act on urgent ones (a child at risk, an intimate image, threats of violence or self-harm) first. If something breaks these rules we remove it — even from a capsule that's still locked — and we may ban the account, permanently. Banned accounts are signed out everywhere and their usernames are never released.
  • Intimate images (Take It Down): if an intimate image of you — or of your child — is on Till Then without consent, anyone can ask us to remove it, without an account, using our safety and removal form. We remove valid requests and known identical copies within 48 hours.
  • Child safety: we report apparent child sexual exploitation to NCMEC as US law requires, and keep the evidence for the time the law requires. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911. You can also report to NCMEC directly at report.cybertip.org.
  • Self-harm: if you or a friend are thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text 988 (US) any time.
  • Law enforcement requests go through the safety form. We respond to valid legal process and, in emergencies involving danger of death or serious injury, may share information without it.

6. How capsules work

  • A capsule stays locked until its reveal date. Before then, nobody can see what's inside — not even the person who made it.
  • You can take back a memory for 24 hours after you add it (or until the capsule opens, if that's sooner). After that it's locked in — that's the point of the app. The only exceptions are a moderator removing something that breaks these rules, and deleting your account (section 7).
  • A group can vote to change its reveal date or open early, and can vote in its chat to start a new capsule. A capsule's chat stays open for the number of days the group chose after it opens, then closes.
  • Once a capsule opens, everyone in it can see and save what's inside. We can't control what people do with things they've saved, so only share what you're comfortable with your capsule seeing forever.
  • Till Then is new. We work hard to keep your memories safe, but we can't promise the service will never be interrupted or that nothing will ever be lost. Save what matters to you once a capsule opens.
